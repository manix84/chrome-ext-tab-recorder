# 🔒 Privacy Policy

## 🧭 Overview

One-Click Tab Recorder is designed with privacy as a core principle. The extension runs entirely inside your browser and does not collect, store, sell, rent, or transmit personal data.

## 📦 Data Collection

This extension does **not collect personal or sensitive user data**.

Specifically:

- 🙅 No personally identifiable information is collected
- 🙅 No browsing history is stored or transmitted
- 🙅 No recorded content is uploaded or shared externally
- 🙅 No analytics, trackers, or advertising SDKs are included

## 🎥 How Recording Works

The extension records the **currently active browser tab** only after you explicitly start recording by clicking the extension icon.

The recording:

- 🎬 Captures video and audio from the active tab
- 💻 Is processed locally in the browser
- 📁 Is saved directly to your device through Chrome downloads

Recorded data is never sent to an external server.

## 🧩 Permissions

### 🎞️ `tabCapture`

Used to capture video and audio from the active browser tab when you start recording.

### 🪟 `offscreen`

Used to create an offscreen document so the extension can access browser media recording APIs that are not available in service workers.

### ⬇️ `downloads`

Used to save the recorded video file to your local device after recording is complete.

### 💾 `storage`

Used to store minimal session state, such as whether a recording is active, so the extension can keep its button state accurate.

## 🌐 Remote Code

This extension does **not use remote code**. All scripts and functionality are bundled inside the extension package.

## 🤝 Data Sharing

No data is shared with third parties.

## 🛡️ Data Security

All recording and processing happens locally in your browser. Since recordings do not leave your device, they are not exposed to external interception or third-party storage by this extension.

## 🎛️ User Control

- ▶️ Recording only starts when you click the extension icon
- ⏹️ Recording can be stopped at any time by clicking the icon again
- 📁 Recordings are saved locally and remain under your control
- 🧹 The extension does not keep copies after saving

## 🔄 Policy Updates

This privacy policy may be updated when the extension changes. Material privacy changes will be documented in `WHATSNEW.md`.
